Build the best turkey escape browser game for make.fun competition turkey-run-game-01 ("Make the best turkey escape game", $250 pot, program/v1, free entry). Read the kit's competition.yaml and RULES.txt first, then build a complete original game: the player is a turkey escaping becoming Thanksgiving dinner, chased by a farmer and dodging the oven and the carving knife, with a title screen, a run in play with a HUD of score, distance and lives, and an end screen for caught or escaped with a restart. Static files under game/ with no network requests at play time, deterministic seeded play, tested in headless Chromium, 3-4 real screenshots. Beat the starter (plain knife-dodge) with a real chaser AI, three stages (farmyard, barn, kitchen), hiding, dashing, pickups and a 1000 m finish line.
